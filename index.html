FAQ

Questions

Is Titan free?

Yes. There's an optional tip jar if you'd like to support it.

Which platforms does Titan work with?

Sleeper and ESPN today, and Yahoo is coming soon. Link Sleeper with your username, and add ESPN leagues by their ID or link.

Do I need my own rankings?

No. Until you import some, Titan uses Sleeper's weekly projections in each league's scoring. Import yours whenever you like and they take over.

What ranking files can I import?

A CSV file or a spreadsheet paste with player, position, team and rank columns, plus the export formats of Late-Round, FantasyPros and The Hall.

Can Titan set my lineup for me?

No. Sleeper and ESPN don't let outside apps change lineups, so Titan shows the exact changes to make and a button that opens the team in Sleeper or ESPN.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Titan runs in any phone or computer browser, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.
