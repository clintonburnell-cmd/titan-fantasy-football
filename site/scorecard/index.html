How Titan's calls have done

Every Tuesday Titan's owner-side report names buy-lows, sell-highs, claims and start ideas. Two finished weeks later, the grader scores each call against what happened: a buy is a hit when he outscored the median of the players the market priced beside him, a sell when he fell short of his, a start idea when it won the week, and a projection by how well it tracked the points against usage alone and against the market's own rank. This page shows those grades as they come in, misses included. The first arrive after week 4.
