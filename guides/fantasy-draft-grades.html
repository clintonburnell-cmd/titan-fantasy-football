Grade your league's fantasy football draft, pick by pick

Last checked September 2026

Most draft grades come out the night of the draft and never change. Titan grades every pick in your own league against the spot it was taken, using today's values, so you can see who really won the draft as the season plays out.

How to see your league's draft

  1. Open Titan and go to League, then Trade.
  2. Pick the league, then tap See the draft on the Draft results line.
  3. The draft opens in a pop-up. Close it with the X, or with your phone's Back button.

How each pick is graded

Every pick is weighed against its spot: what that pick would get if the draft were held again today, with every player going in order of value. A pick that beats its spot by a lot is a steal, marked in green. One that fell well short is a reach, marked in amber.

The value is Titan's own: a player's projected points this season above a replacement starter at his position, in your league's scoring, from Sleeper's season projections. As the projections change through the season, so do the grades. Keepers aren't graded, since nobody chose them at that spot, and in an auction the spots follow price, so the priciest player should be the most valuable.

Team grades

Each team's picks add up to a total against their spots, and teams are graded A+ to D by how far that total sits from the rest of the league. Every team shows its best pick and its biggest reach. Tap a team to see all its picks, each with where it went at its position against where it ranks there now, like "RB10 taken, RB1 now".

The draft board

Board shows the whole draft round by round, a column for each team, with the steals in green and the reaches in amber. A traded pick says which team made it. On a phone the board scrolls sideways.

Dynasty rookie drafts

Titan's values count this season only, so rookies who won't start this year look worth almost nothing. When most of the players in a draft aren't projected to start this season, Titan shows the board without grades, because the grades would be noise.

Thinking about a trade with the team that drafted best? Titan's trade help weighs it in the same place.
