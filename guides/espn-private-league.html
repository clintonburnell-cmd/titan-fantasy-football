How to add your ESPN fantasy football league, public or private

Titan reads ESPN leagues by their league ID. Public leagues need nothing else. Private leagues also need your ESPN login, which Titan keeps in your own account.

Find your league ID

Open your league on fantasy.espn.com. The address in your browser contains leagueId= followed by a number, like leagueId=123456789. You can paste that whole link into Titan, or just the number.

Add it in Titan

  1. Open Titan and go to Settings, then Link more leagues?, then ESPN. (New to Titan? On the first screen, tap Add an ESPN league instead.)
  2. Paste the league link or ID.
  3. Pick your team from the list. Titan loads the league with its own lineup spots and scoring.

If the league is public, you're done. If ESPN says it's private, Titan asks for your ESPN login.

Private leagues: your ESPN login

ESPN only shows a private league to its members, so Titan needs the two cookies ESPN's website uses to keep you signed in: espn_s2 and SWID. First, sign in to Titan with Google (Settings, then Sync across your devices), because the login is saved to your own Titan account.

To find the two values, on a computer:

  1. Sign in at fantasy.espn.com in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Open the developer tools: F12 on Windows, or Cmd + Option + I on a Mac.
  3. Go to Application, then Cookies, then https://fantasy.espn.com. (In Firefox it's Storage, then Cookies.)
  4. Find espn_s2 and SWID and copy each value.
  5. Paste them into Titan's ESPN login form and save.

How Titan keeps your ESPN login

  • It's saved only to your Titan account, which only you can read.
  • Titan's server uses it for one thing: reading your ESPN leagues, which a browser can't do for a private league.
  • The app never shows it again, and Settings → Link more leagues? → ESPN → Remove my ESPN login deletes it.
  • Because those cookies keep you signed in to ESPN, signing out of ESPN on every device also makes a saved copy stop working.

The privacy policy has the details.

What you get

ESPN leagues get everything Sleeper leagues do: start/sit calls from your rankings, the changes to make with an Open in ESPN button, live scores, your weekly matchup with a chance to win, and weekly Results. See how to import your rankings to put your own calls behind it.
