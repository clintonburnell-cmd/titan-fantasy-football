Start/sit help for all your Sleeper leagues

Link your Sleeper username once and Titan checks every lineup you have, in every league, against your rankings. No password, and it works for any Sleeper account.

How to link Sleeper

  1. Open Titan.
  2. Type your Sleeper username and tap Link Sleeper account.
  3. That's it. Titan finds every league you're in this season, with each league's own lineup spots and scoring.

Best-ball leagues start switched off, because Sleeper sets those lineups itself. You can switch any league on or off in Settings. Titan only reads what your Sleeper profile already shows publicly.

What each call means

  • OK: the right player is in this spot.
  • Swap out: someone on your bench ranks higher, and Titan lists the exact change.
  • Do not start: he's ruled out, doubtful or on IR.
  • On bye: his team doesn't play this week, so his backup goes in.
  • Locked: his game has kicked off, so he can't be moved, and Titan never suggests moving him.
  • Fill slot: the spot is empty.

The calls come from the rankings you import (see how to import rankings), or from Sleeper's weekly projections until you do.

Later kickoffs go in your FLEX

When two starters could trade spots, Titan puts the one who plays later in the FLEX and the earlier one in his own position's spot. If a late game brings a surprise scratch, you can still swap the FLEX for anyone on your bench. When your lineup has them the other way round, Titan lists it as one change.

Making the changes

Sleeper doesn't let outside apps change lineups, so Titan shows you what to change and gives every league an Open in Sleeper button that goes straight to that team.

More for people in several leagues

  • Matchup: your lineup and your opponent's in every league, with the score and your chance to win.
  • Live scores: points update about every minute while games are on.
  • Exposure: which players you own on more than one team.
  • Byes: every upcoming bye week, league by league, with the positions you'll need.
  • Results: how each week went, against the projection and against what your rankings would have started.
