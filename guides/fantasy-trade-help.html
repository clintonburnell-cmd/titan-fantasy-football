Weigh any trade in your fantasy football league

Titan's Trade tab works inside your own league: the real rosters, the league's real format, and both starting lineups. Tap the players on each side and it tells you who wins and what the trade does to both teams.

How to weigh a trade

  1. Open Titan and go to League, then Trade.
  2. Pick the league and a trade partner.
  3. Tap the players you'd give from your roster and the ones you'd get from theirs.

Titan says who wins the trade, or that it's fair when the two sides are within 5% of each other, and how each team's value changes in percent. When it's lopsided, it suggests players that would even it out. It also shows each team's best starting lineup by this week's projections, before and after, so you can see what the trade does to Sunday.

Where the values come from

Who wins is weighed with trade values from FantasyCalc, for your league's format: redraft or dynasty, one QB or superflex, the number of teams, and PPR. When a trade is two players for one, the side getting fewer players also gets credit for the roster spot it frees. The number beside each player is Titan's own value: his projected points this season above a replacement starter at his position, in your league's scoring. Titan isn't affiliated with FantasyCalc.

Finding a trade worth making

  • Trade ideas: tap Find trades for fair one- and two-player deals that make your starting lineup stronger, and your partner's too where possible.
  • Who has him?: search for any player to see which team has him, or whether he's a free agent, then tap Trade for him to start the trade.
  • Where you both stand: once you pick a partner, Titan shows where each of you is deep or thin by position, and calls out the good fits, like their depth at running back filling your hole there.

Dynasty leagues

In dynasty Sleeper leagues, each team's picks for the next three drafts can go in the trade too, with who owns which pick taken from Sleeper's traded picks. Titan's own value beside each player counts this season only, so it doesn't account for age or future seasons.

Sending the offer

Sleeper and ESPN don't let outside apps fill in a trade offer, so Copy and open copies the trade as a line of text and opens your team on the site. Start the trade there, add the same players, and paste the text as a note if you like.

Curious who drafted best in your league? Draft results grade every pick, right from the Trade tab.
