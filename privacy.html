Privacy

Last updated September 10, 2026.

What Titan stores

  • The Sleeper username you link, and the league switches you choose.
  • The rankings you import.
  • A copy of your last refresh (rosters, injuries and game locks) so the app opens instantly.
  • A trimmed copy of Sleeper's public player list (names, positions and teams).

All of it is kept in your browser's storage on your own device. None of it is sent to Titan or anyone else. Clearing your browser data, or tapping Switch account in Settings, removes it.

Who Titan talks to

Your device reads your leagues directly from Sleeper's public API (api.sleeper.app), the same information anyone can see on your public Sleeper profile. Titan has no password for your account and cannot change your lineups, make claims or trade. Profile pictures load from Sleeper's image server.

What Titan doesn't do

  • No accounts, no sign-up and no server that collects your data.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No sharing or selling of anything.

Changes

If Titan adds syncing across your devices, this page will say exactly what is stored and where before that feature ships.
