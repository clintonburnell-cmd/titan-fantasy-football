What's in each email

  • Grab before waivers run: players whose role just grew, with the snap and target numbers to back it up.
  • Buy low: players getting the work but not the points yet. That usually evens out.
  • Sell high: players scoring well above their usage, often on touchdowns that don't last.
  • Role watch: the biggest jumps in snap share and targets, so you spot the next breakout early.

Where the picks come from

Every pick comes from usage, not hype: snap share, targets, carries, red-zone looks and expected fantasy points (what a player's opportunities usually score), from the free, open NFL data at nflverse. It's the same engine behind Titan's own waiver and trade tools.
